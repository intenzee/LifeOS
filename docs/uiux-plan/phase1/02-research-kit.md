# Phase 1 · Research kit (owner)

Only the owner can produce these. The team will not make up diary entries or ratings. This page makes the owner's part quick to do.

## 1. Five-day usage diary

Keep one Apple Note called **"LifeOS diary"**. Each time something is slow, confusing or ugly, add a line in this form:

```
<time> · <screen> · <what happened> · <how it felt, 1 to 5>
```

At the end of each day, answer the seven questions from Phase 1 §4.1. Copy this block into the note:

```
Day N (date)
1. When I thought about food/exercise/health, and whether I opened LifeOS:
2. Meals logged, seconds each, what was annoying:
3. Meals NOT logged, and why:
4. Did my Apple Watch workout show up anywhere useful? What did I expect?
5. Which Today number did I look at? Did I understand it?
6. What would I let an assistant log without asking?
7. Which other app felt premium today, and exactly why:
```

**Things to watch for**, taken from the audit (each one needs your real-use confirmation):

| Audit ID | Watch for |
|---|---|
| A2 | Do you know why the ring says what it says? Can you explain the number after a workout? |
| A18 | Does water progress look different in different places? |
| A19 | Does a streak chip ever light up for a goal you didn't hit? |
| A20 | After an AI scan, did changing the unit give a silly calorie number? |
| A23 | Do you ever tap the wrong tab, or fail to find Reminders? |
| A25 | Do you bother toggling "high protein", and does it match what you ate? |
| A13 | Use the app in light mode for one day. What is unreadable? |

**Synthesis after day 5** (one page, in `02-research-kit.md` §3 below):
- 5 to 8 jobs to be done, each written as "When … I want to … so I can …"
- Top 5 friction points, ranked by how often they happened multiplied by how much they hurt
- Your own vocabulary: the words you actually use ("usual breakfast", "gym", "chai")

**Optional:** ask 2 or 3 friends, on a free video call, two questions: "How many calories are left?" (time it, 5 s) and "Which app on your phone feels most premium, and why?"
